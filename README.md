# Lost and Found System

A web-based lost and found management system developed for Ateneo de Naga University to modernize the process of reporting, tracking, and recovering lost items on campus. The platform streamlines communication between students, faculty, staff, and the Office of Student Affairs (OSA) through a centralized and searchable system.
